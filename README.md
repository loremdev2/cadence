# Cadence

Cadence is an AI calendar assistant designed to make a busy workweek easier to manage. It helps people find room for focused work, prepare for meetings, resolve schedule conflicts, and plan meetings with clear control over every change.

## Explore the demo

Open the app and choose **Explore the demo** to try Cadence with a sample work calendar. You can ask for focus time or describe a meeting in your own words. Cadence suggests a time, lets you review and edit the details, and waits for your confirmation before making a change.

Try asking: “Find 90 minutes for focus before Thursday, avoiding mornings.”

The guided tour shows where the main features live and how to use them.

## What Cadence can do

- Find focus time that fits around existing meetings and personal scheduling preferences.
- Draft meetings from a natural-language request, with editable time, duration, agenda, notes, location, and attendees.
- Prepare concise meeting briefs.
- Highlight conflicts and suggest alternative times.
- Explain schedule suggestions so you can decide what works for you.
- Show the calendar in day or week view, with light and dark themes.

## Calendar access

The sample calendar is available to everyone and does not use real calendar data. Connecting a Google Calendar is currently limited to approved testers while Google reviews the app’s verification and privacy requirements. If you would like to try Cadence with your own calendar, contact Aman with the email address you want added as a tester.
