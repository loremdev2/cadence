# Brag Plan: Cadence

## What is this app?
Cadence reads a busy calendar, finds practical room for focus and meetings, and lets the user review every change before it is written.

## The angle
The week looks packed, but the grid is not the whole story. Cadence turns a plain-language request into a calendar-aware option and a reviewable event draft, then waits for the user's confirmation.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
A tight crop of a busy week grid. Headline: “Your calendar is full.” One beat later: “Your week still has options.”

## Key moments (the middle)
- Show the Cadence week view with focus blocks and open time clearly marked.
- Show a focus suggestion with a concise reason alongside the week.
- Type “Schedule a meeting Thursday at 3pm for 44 minutes” into the Assistant; reveal the event draft editor with editable duration, agenda, notes, location, and optional attendees.

## Outro / punchline
The week view clears around one protected focus block. Close on “Make room for the work that matters.” and the Cadence wordmark.

## User flow worth showing
Open the demo week → ask Cadence to schedule a 44-minute Thursday meeting → review the time and editable event details before confirming.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet, confident product film with a small moment of relief
- Interpretation: restrained typography and motion; make the calendar itself the visual, and let the reviewed-before-write behavior carry the story.

## Format: landscape — 1920×1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #FAF9F5
- Accent: #1E3D33
- Text: #191816
- Display font: Instrument Serif
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the week grid beside the compact Cadence Assistant and its reasoned time suggestions.

## Share copy (draft)
Cadence turns a crowded calendar into a week you can work with—and waits for your say before making changes.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, understated momentum
- Music: “Happy Beats / Business Moves” vol. 12, bundled with Brag
- Music treatment: begin gently under the hook, keep the bed at a restrained level, and resolve with the wordmark.
- Music cue guidance: bundled cue preset at 109.96 BPM; strong cues at 8.74s, 13.11s, and 17.47s. Use nearby beats only where they support legibility.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none
- SFX posture: sparse, soft UI sounds on typing and the option/confirmation reveal.
- Audio-coupled moments: keyboard ticks while the request appears; a quiet click as the suggestion settles; one soft confirmation accent.
- Restraint rule: never make the interface feel urgent or imply that Cadence changes a calendar without approval.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — A packed week — 3s
The Cadence calendar grid fills the frame with a few sample events. Copy appears: “Your calendar is full.” Let one small open space remain visible.
Sequential/interaction: the week columns settle into place, then the headline appears.
Audio intent: establish calm confidence, not stress.
Audio-coupled idea: one light interface tap when the grid settles.
Music: warm, restrained bed from the opening.
Transition mood: soft push → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Options — 3s
Reveal the complete week view and Cadence Assistant beside it. Headline: “Your week still has options.” Focus time and open time are visibly distinct.
Sequential/interaction: the assistant panel slides into view after the calendar.
Audio intent: a gentle lift.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft card arrival on the reveal.
Music: continue the same bed.
Transition mood: clean slide → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Ask — 4s
Show the real Assistant composer. Type “Schedule a meeting Thursday at 3pm for 44 minutes.” The request stays on screen long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: simulate typing in the composer; the response appears after the full prompt.
Audio intent: make the interaction feel tangible.
Audio-coupled idea: sparse keyboard ticks while typing, then stop.
Music: steady under the interaction.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Review — 6s
Show the event draft at Thursday, 3:00 PM, with the custom 44-minute duration and optional agenda, notes, location, and attendee fields. The confirmation control is visible; do not imply it was pressed.
Sequential/interaction: suggestion first, editable details second, confirmation control last.
Audio intent: confidence through control.
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet click for the suggestion and one soft accent on the confirmation control.
Music: align the detail reveal near the 13.11s cue without rushing text.
Transition mood: gentle wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Make room — 4s
Return to the week view with a focus block called out. Close on “Make room for the work that matters.” and the Cadence wordmark.
Sequential/interaction: the focus block settles, then the line and wordmark arrive.
Audio intent: small, satisfying resolution.
Audio-coupled idea: quiet logo accent near the 17.47s cue.
Music: resolve and fade at 20s.
Transition mood: soft fade → End

**Music mood for this video:** warm and polished
**Audio summary:** a quiet upbeat bed supports a clear progression from calendar pressure to a user-approved plan, with only a few soft interface accents.
